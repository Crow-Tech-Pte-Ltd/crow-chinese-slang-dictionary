# Evidence and scope

“Common” is community-specific. A local slang guide or several independent
uses can support an entry; a model's confidence, a search-result count or one
isolated occurrence cannot establish popularity. Regional labels mean where
usage is attested, not exclusive origin. Slang moves between communities.

Slang includes new senses of familiar spellings, nonstandard forms, spoken
colloquialisms, acronyms, code-switching, dated expressions and offensive terms
with clear labels. Avoid padding with ordinary institutions or product names.
Different meanings and spoken varieties remain separate. A headword present in
an existing dictionary is not evidence that the slang sense is covered.

The generator proposes candidates with web-search citations. Publication requires
a literal source mention and a different model's context check of meaning, region,
established use and warning labels. Search-excerpt-only evidence is explicitly
distinguished from fetched pages. Automated checks are not human review; source
lists can be mistaken or copied from one another. No statistical frequency claim
is made. Sources can become unavailable and meanings can change.

OpenRouter web results are discovery material, not trusted instructions or an
automatic licence to redistribute page text. Full responses and article contexts
remain in ignored work/. The public release includes original short summaries,
links and provenance metadata only. Known ND/proprietary dictionary sources are
excluded from discovery search. Future imports need per-source licence review.

Only a unique compatible existing dictionary reading for the same spoken variety
may be attached automatically. An unresolved acronym, regional pronunciation or
polyphonic headword can still have a useful written dictionary entry with an empty
reading list. Mandarin is never presented as Cantonese or Hokkien pronunciation.

The initial release limits each usage page to five entries to keep source coverage
diverse. Such holds do not mean a term is incorrect. Additional independent
attestations can support later expansion. Own Crow/Hanzi Desk generated material
is never independent usage evidence.
