# Crow Chinese Slang Dictionary

- Describe attested informal use; do not enforce a prestige standard or exclude vulgarity solely for being vulgar.
- Region means community of attestation, not exclusive origin or universal usage.
- Preserve separate senses, spoken varieties, source-backed readings and warning labels.
- Never invent pronunciation or infer Jyutping from Mandarin characters. Unknown readings stay empty.
- OpenRouter candidate suggestions and same-model reviews are not independent evidence.
- Retain source URLs, access dates, checksums, machine-review labels and the persistent budget ledger.
- Raw page text, API responses, credentials and unreviewed drafts stay ignored in work/ or private/.
- Do not feed known ND dictionary definitions into models or relicense proprietary dictionary text.
- Code is MIT; contributed/compatible derivative entry data is CC BY-SA 4.0, with upstream rights retained.
- Keep Hanzi Desk, its native app, original dictionary and audio corpus read-only for this dataset task.
- No website deployment or audio generation is authorized by corpus preparation alone.
- Crow/Hanzi Desk generated material is coverage data, never independent primary usage evidence.
