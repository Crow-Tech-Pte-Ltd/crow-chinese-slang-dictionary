Contributed original summaries and compatible derived entry data are offered
under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Reuse, modification and commercial use are permitted with attribution, retained
notices and ShareAlike for adaptations. Pipeline code is MIT.

Imported English Wiktionary definitions retain CC BY-SA 4.0 and credit to
Wiktionary contributors; Kaikki.org / Wiktextract supplied the extraction.
Individual entries retain page URLs, source sense identifiers, labels and snapshot
checksums. The models select these senses; they do not author the imported text.
See data/NOTICE.txt and each sourceImport record.

Source pages retain their own copyrights. URLs, access dates and evidence
metadata do not relicense the underlying articles. Raw article text, copied
proprietary definitions, personal posts and model responses are not distributed.
Machine-written summaries are labelled; paraphrasing never automatically removes
upstream rights. Existing dictionary readings retain source credits and licences.
Known ND dictionary definitions are excluded from model input and derived entries.

These are descriptive records of informal language, not endorsements or claims
that every community member uses a term. No warranty of completeness, universal
popularity, correct pronunciation or human review is made.
